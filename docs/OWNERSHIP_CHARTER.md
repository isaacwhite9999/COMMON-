# COMMON — Ownership charter

“We want the people to own the companies on the market, not the companies to own the people.”

COMMON is a participatory ownership network designed to broaden economic agency, public-company ownership, financial literacy and lawful shareholder participation. It does not promise returns or a particular redistribution of ownership.

## Product invariants

- Membership, investment and participation are voluntary; preserve individual agency, privacy and exit rights.
- Community mandates, conflicts, decision rules, amendments and decision records must be transparent to authorized participants.
- Distinguish personal ownership, beneficial ownership, legal voting authority, nonbinding community polls and simulations.
- Design governance against capture by administrators, large contributors, bots and coordinated abuse. No purchased platform-wide governance influence.
- Research must be evidence-based, reproducible, dated and accessible at beginner and professional levels.
- Prohibit manipulation, misleading financial claims, harassment and undisclosed promotion.
- Build provider contracts and simulated workflows now; real custody, orders, transfers, offerings and proxy submissions stay disabled until applicable provider, security and legal gates pass.
- No fake quotes, financial balances, research results, member counts or claims of institutional equivalence.

## Design and implementation

Premium, dense, configurable financial workspace with persistent navigation, nested interactions and responsive layouts. Preserve existing tested subsystems. Implement complete vertical workflows, authorization, data persistence, errors and accessibility. Record competitor observations separately from proposed features. Use isolated branches, regression tests and review before release.

Status labels: implemented and tested; implemented but unverified; existing linked subsystem; planned; provider/approval gated.
