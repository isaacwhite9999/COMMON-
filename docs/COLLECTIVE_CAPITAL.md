# COMMON collective capital
## Product purpose
Enable voluntary trusted circles to acquire meaningful ownership through lawful collective investment arrangements, while preserving member independence and protecting against concentration of power.
## Development increment
The authenticated circle includes Capital & treasury: overview, equal-contribution scenario planner, acquisition budget and reserve model, fee assumptions, downside stress case, illustrative quorum and approval rules, JSON export and publication of a nonbinding research poll within private circles. No actual member financial details are entered. Scenario drafts live in memory until navigation away from the application; shared research polls use the existing community proposal database and membership checks. No pledge, subscription, offer, deposit, fund interest, actual balance or authorization is created. The UI is not an authorization boundary for financial activity; all actual financial activity remains absent.
## Target live architecture (not yet implemented)
- Legal entity per eligible circle with governing documents, jurisdiction, beneficial ownership and legal review; distinct from the social community.
- Account onboarding, eligibility checks, member agreements, identity verification and financial partner approval.
- Partner-held cash and securities; deposit/withdrawal intents, idempotency and verified webhooks; immutable double-entry journal, reconciliation, statements and exception queues.
- Member capital accounts, subscriptions/redemptions, valuation/NAV, unit or capital-account accounting, applicable tax records, fee disclosures and audit trail.
- Versioned mandate, risk limits, conflicts and abstention, voting eligibility snapshot, quorum, approval, revocation, independent sign-off, approved-order state machine; no execution until authorized by the legal entity and provider.
- Portfolio orders and settlement through a permitted broker; positions, corporate actions, dividends, transfers and reconciliation.
- Separate workflows for public securities, private offerings and real assets, with asset-specific eligibility, legal review and liquidity controls. No coordination intended to artificially move security prices.
- No admin override of provider gating. No actual deposits, withdrawals, trading or fundraising until legal and security review, partner agreements and integration tests are complete.

## External recordkeeping pilot (development only)
The external journal is a manual, aggregate USD bookkeeping feature, distinct from simulated capital planning and from any financial transfer. The draft schema stores balanced immutable entries with an internal reference, record date and recorder identity, with exact reversals and membership-scoped access. It is not a member capital-account system, custody ledger, verified provider reconciliation, NAV or accounting advice. Draft SQL is tested only in the disposable CI database; before enabling `externalLedgerEnabled`, generate and review a proper migration, test it against staging with security advisors and approve the private-circle pilot. Never turn the flag on in production until these gates are satisfied. Real financial transactions remain unavailable. Decision detail screens use existing authenticated opinion polls and aggregate tallies; they do not authorize orders or payments.

### Journal period statement and manual cash comparison

In the disposable journal pilot, open **Capital & treasury → External journal**. A current private-circle member can prepare an aggregate USD statement for an inclusive reporting period of up to 366 days. The report includes opening balances, the period's debits and credits, and closing balances on each account's normal side. An append-only reversal changes the book balances on its own record date. Each report comes from one database snapshot. The statement rejects a request above 10,000 evidence entries through the end date instead of returning incomplete figures.

Users can enter an independently observed aggregate closing cash amount and see the precise difference between journal cash and that entered amount. The comparison stays in browser memory. An explicit JSON export contains the statement, the selected circle and period, the UTC-capable generation timestamp, the evidence fingerprint, and any manual comparison. Export rechecks current membership and the fingerprint. A later entry with an earlier record date can change a statement, so prepare it again before exporting. Already saved exports retain the amounts and evidence fingerprint of that report. The fingerprint covers sorted journal IDs, record dates and lines. It does not validate the original source or preserve the underlying evidence rows.

The read RPC uses the caller's existing journal RLS and requires current private-circle membership. Anonymous, public-circle and former-member requests fail. Existing journal posting and reversal permissions are unchanged. The comparison does not establish custody, ownership, NAV, accounting sign-off or a verified balance. It makes no provider calls and stores no statement or external cash value in the database.

Per-member equity accounts, statements from verified providers, and independent financial approval remain open. The SQL remains a development-only draft and the existing production flag remains disabled.
