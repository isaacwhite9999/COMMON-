Read AGENTS.md, docs/COLLECTIVE_CAPITAL.md and docs/SECURITY_AND_REGULATORY_GATES.md. Separate social membership, legal entity, eligible ownership, nonbinding opinion and binding authority. Use integer minor units, balanced journals, append-only corrections, idempotency, verified provider events, reconciliation and authorization checks. Test duplicate/out-of-order events, access isolation and failure recovery. Do not treat manual records as verified balances. No real money movement, custody, investment offers or execution until approved legal and provider gates. Never use production funds or credentials in tests.
